; Vole Machine Program: Combined OR and AND Operations
; Purpose: Perform OR followed by AND and display the final result.

203C ; Load immediate value 3C into register 0
210F ; Load immediate value 0F into register 1
7221 ; OR registers 2 and 1, put result in register 2
8321 ; AND registers 2 and 1, put result in register 3
33FF ; Store register 3 to address FF (screen output)
C000 ; Halt execution
